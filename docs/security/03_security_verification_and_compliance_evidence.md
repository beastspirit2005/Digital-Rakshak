## 2. Statutory Demarcation & Legal Review

### 2.1 Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023 — Section 63
* **Statutory Requirement**: Section 63 governs the admissibility of electronic records in judicial proceedings, specifying conditions regarding regular production, ordinary course of activity, and operating integrity.
* **Technical Capability**: The platform implements a **Sequential Cryptographic Hash Chain**:
  $$H_n = \text{SHA256}(H_{n-1} \parallel \text{Event}_n)$$
  coupled with HMAC-SHA256 digital checkpoint signing.
* **Legal Demarcation**: Technical hash chaining proves **data immutability and tamper-detection**. It does not constitute self-executing statutory certification. Official court admissibility requires an Investigating Officer or system custodian to sign the Section 63(4) Certificate of Admissibility affidavit generated by the system.

### 2.2 Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023 — Section 94
* **Statutory Requirement**: Section 94 empowers police officers to issue written orders for the production of digital documents, electronic devices, or banking records necessary for investigation.
* **Technical Capability**: The platform provides formatted electronic requisition notices and audit logs capturing time of dispatch, recipient bank/PSP endpoint, and delivery confirmation.
* **Legal Demarcation**: While the platform automates requisition generation and tracks dispatch via the Transactional Outbox, actual fund freezes remain subject to the recipient bank's internal statutory compliance and judicial confirmation.
